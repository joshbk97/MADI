Pneumonia Detection

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, causing cough with phlegm or pus, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing. Early diagnosis via chest X-rays is critical for effective treatment.

Symptoms

The signs and symptoms of pneumonia vary from mild to severe and may include:

  • Chest pain when you breathe or cough
  • Confusion or changes in mental awareness
  • Cough, which may produce phlegm
  • Fatigue and fever, sweating and shaking chills
  • Lower than normal body temperature (in adults older than 65)
  • Nausea, vomiting or diarrhea
  • Shortness of breath
