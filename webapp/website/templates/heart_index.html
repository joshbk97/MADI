Heart Disease

Heart disease describes a range of conditions that affect your heart, including blood vessel disease, heart rhythm problems, and heart defects. Many forms of heart disease can be prevented or treated with healthy lifestyle choices if detected early.

Symptoms

Common heart attack signs and symptoms include:

  • Pressure, tightness, pain, or a squeezing sensation in the chest
  • Pain spreading to your neck, jaw or back
  • Nausea, indigestion, heartburn or abdominal pain
  • Shortness of breath and cold sweat
  • Fatigue and lightheadedness
